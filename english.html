THE GAME

YOUR GARAGE.
YOUR NEXT PROJECT.

Hands-on repairs, driving and life in the Finnish countryside.

The game combines removable vehicle parts, bolts, tools and customer repair jobs. Part condition and installation affect the car. The fictional economy uses in-game CR credits.

Our goal is an open countryside with a story you can follow alongside your own projects. Esko’s inheritance, a neglected farm and an unfinished rally car form the campaign’s starting point. Summer and winter bring different road conditions and workshop chores, including firewood.

The mechanics, world, handling, graphics and character models are still being developed. Final content and system requirements are not confirmed.

DEVELOPMENT STATUS / 7 NOVEMBER 2026

STILL IN
THE WORKSHOP.

The terrain is now real Central Finland, built from the National Land Survey of Finland open data (CC BY 4.0): hills, hollows, ten lakes, fields and bogs. The world does not match any real place. Roads rise and fall with the land and have ditches, shoulders and slopes, with lupins and fireweed on the verges and wooden power poles along the main roads. Ditches are a real hazard: a car sliding in slowly can creep back out, but one that hits a ditch fast gets damaged and can fly over it into the forest. The world is about 4.8 × 4.8 km of countryside with roughly 21 km of roads, a church village, a lakeside village, a sawmill, a gravel pit and a bog. The new areas have no jobs or shops yet. Ideas from our Facebook followers are in too: a check-engine light with blink codes, frost potholes, seized rusty bolts, a back-lot car seller and customers who pay partly in moonshine. The cars and engine bay were rebuilt earlier. Earlier work updated the landscape, seasonal surfaces, sky, workshop objects and material finishes. Characters, trees and many vehicle and building details still need further art work.
